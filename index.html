01 / WHO WE ARE

Community progress
starts with people.

Queens Community Impact Network (QCIN) is a volunteer-led community service group focused on practical education, small-business development, and civic-minded collaboration.

Our goal is simple: make useful skills, resources, and connections more accessible to the people and businesses that make Queens thrive.

Find your place in our network ↗

02 / OUR FOCUS

Practical support.
Meaningful impact.

We focus on skills and services that help individuals and neighborhood businesses move forward.

↗
01

Personal Finance Education

Accessible workshops and resources that help community members make informed everyday financial decisions.

  • Budgeting and saving basics
  • Understanding credit and debt
  • Financial goal setting
  • Banking and money management literacy
◎
02

Small Business Outreach

Helping local entrepreneurs strengthen their visibility and build connections with customers and their communities.

  • Community awareness campaigns
  • Social media and digital presence guidance
  • Local event and promotion support
  • Customer engagement ideas
▦
03

Business Resource Support

Sharing practical tools and volunteer skills to help neighborhood businesses work more effectively.

  • Basic marketing and branding guidance
  • Digital literacy and productivity tools
  • Business planning fundamentals
  • Resource referrals and networking

Programs and support activities are developed based on volunteer capacity and community needs. Financial education is general information, not individualized financial advice.
